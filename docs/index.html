Typical Stickman Vids

Short animated stickman videos for YouTube Shorts: everyday science, money explained with real numbers, and story series. Every fact is checked against a source listed in the video description.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@typicalstickmanvids

About this app

"Stickman Shorts Uploader" is a small private tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
