A man sold the Eiffel Tower. Then he came back and sold it again.

Paris, nineteen twenty-five. The tower is rusting, and repairs cost a fortune.

A con man named Victor Lustig poses as a government official. He invites six scrap dealers to a luxury hotel and whispers: the tower is coming down, and the metal goes to the highest bidder.

One dealer pays. Lustig is on a train to Austria with the money.

But the buyer never calls the police. He's too embarrassed.

So six months later, Lustig comes back. Same speech. New buyer.

And the tower? Still standing. Still not for sale.
