## Current series: FLOOR 13 (6 parts) — music preset: `mystery`
**Premise:** You live on floor 12 of an apartment block. One night a glowing "13" button appears in the
elevator. Floor 13 is a mirror copy of your floor where everything is backwards — and someone there
already knows you. PG mystery, spooky-not-scary, no gore, no real brands.

**Characters (keep these looks in every episode):**
- **You** — `hero()` default: blue hoodie `#3b82f6`, dark pants `#334155`, no hair (hood).
- **Mia** — your neighbour from 12B, ~same age, brave and sarcastic. `stick()` shirt `#16a34a`,

- **Echo** — the floor-13 copy of you. Same as You but hoodie grey `#9ca3af`, mouth 'smirk', always
  `flip: true` (mirrored). Speaks in your voice. Wants to swap places with you.

**Setting:** Elevator (steel walls `#94a3b8`, button panel with 12 and glowing 13), hallway with one
potted plant, red carpet `#b91c1c`, beige walls, doors with brass numbers. On floor 13 every number
and sign is mirrored (draw text with `ctx.scale(-1, 1)`), lights flicker slightly.

**Arc:**
1. The 13 button appears. You press it. Floor 13 = your hallway but backwards. Someone calls your name — in your own voice.
2. You follow the voice to door "B21" (12B backwards). Inside: your room, mirrored. On the desk a note in your handwriting: "DON'T TRUST THE ELEVATOR." Cliff: footsteps behind you.
3. It's Mia, stuck here for a week. Downstairs "someone" has been living her life, so nobody missed her. Cliff: the elevator doors close by themselves and it leaves without you.
4. Echo appears: friendly, helpful, a bit too perfect. Offers to show the way back. Cliff: Mia whispers "That's how it got me."
5. Stairwell escape: numbers count wrong; the backwards rule — to go down you have to go up. Cliff: the lights die and Echo is waiting at the stairwell door.
6. Finale: you press the panel backwards (2-1) so the elevator obeys the mirror rule; you and Mia make it to 12, Echo is left behind the closing doors. Back home all normal… but the elevator panel now has a new button: 14. THE END.
