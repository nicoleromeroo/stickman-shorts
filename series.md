## Finished series: RESPAWN (6 parts) — music preset: `tense` for action, `mystery` allowed for quiet parts
**Premise:** You wake up and your normal life has turned into a video game: a health bar with 3 hearts
floats above your head, people repeat the same lines like NPCs, quest boxes pop up in front of your face.
Lose all hearts and you respawn in the morning, but something in the world is deleted each time.
PG, video-game style, no gore (losing a heart = heart icon cracks + red flash, nothing more).

**Characters (keep these looks in every episode):**
- **You** — `hero()` default: blue hoodie `#3b82f6`, dark pants `#334155`. 3 heart icons above the head (`hearts()` in the Part 1 html).
- **Mom (NPC)** — `stick()` shirt `#f472b6`, hair `#7c2d12`, apron. Name tag "MOM · NPC". Loops "Breakfast is ready!".
- **Neighbour (NPC)** — `stick()` shirt `#16a34a`, hair `#9ca3af` (grey), watering can, waters the same flower forever.
- **Kai** — the other real player (from Part 2). `stick()` shirt `#f97316` (orange hoodie, hood), pants `#1f2937`,
  RED hearts above his head. Sarcastic, has respawned "300 times".
- **The Hall Monitor** (boss, Part 4) — very tall faceless stick figure in a grey uniform `#64748b`, glowing yellow
  sash, only says "No running."

**Game UI style:** dark navy panels `#0f172a` with white border, Nunito 900 text, a yellow "!" quest icon,
glitch effect = RGB-split offset copies of the frame for 0.2 s.

**Arc:**
1. You wake up with a health bar. Mom loops "Breakfast is ready!", the neighbour waters the same flower. Quest box: "Survive the school day." You trip on the stairs and lose a heart. Cliff: a whisper — "Two lives left."
2. School: classmates repeat lines, the teacher says "Level 2 unlocked". Behind the gym a glowing door. Cliff: someone else with a health bar — red hearts — is standing in front of it (Kai).
3. Kai explains the rules: lose all hearts → respawn in the morning, but each respawn deletes something. Cliff: you look out of the school window — your house is gone from the street.
4. Boss level: the Hall Monitor chases you both through endless corridors; you grab a floating heart. Cliff: Kai loses his last heart and fades out.
5. You respawn alone. Kai doesn't remember you. In your desk: a notebook full of maps and rules — "save file" — in YOUR handwriting, dated 300 days ago. Cliff: you're the one who has been looping, not Kai.
6. Finale: the game only follows its script, so you beat it by doing something unscripted — you help an NPC (Mom) instead of finishing the quest. "GAME COMPLETE". You wake up, no health bar, normal morning… then your phone shows "NEW GAME+ ?  YES / NO". THE END.

YES / NO" (comment prompt: would you press yes?). THE END — series finished; next run starts a new series.


## Current series: LOW BATTERY (6 parts, from 2026-11-21) — music preset: `mystery` (all episodes)
**Premise:** It's 11:58 PM, your phone is at 5 %, and you get texts from your own number warning you about
things before they happen. At midnight the whole town freezes; only you (and later Mia) can still move while the
battery counts down. A creepy-but-PG mystery: no gore, no violence; the threat is "The Static", a shadow that copies
your voice. Each episode ends with a choice question for the comments ("Would you open the door?").

**Characters (keep these looks in every episode):**
- **You** — `hero()` default: blue hoodie `#3b82f6`, dark pants `#334155`. Phone always in hand, battery % shown big on screen.
- **Mia** (from Part 2/3) — the only other person awake. `stick()` yellow raincoat shirt `#facc15` with hood, pants
  `#1f2937`, black hair `#111827`. Calm, brave, carries a flashlight. Her phone also gets texts from her own number.
- **The Static** (from Part 1, seen in Part 3) — a tall dark silhouette `#0b1020` with flickering white static
  noise inside and two small white dot eyes; it copies voices (yours first). Never touches anyone; it only knocks,
  whispers and blocks the way.

**Text style:** messages from "ME" = green bubbles `#22c55e` on a dark phone screen, sender label "ME", battery icon
top right. Night palette: navy `#0b1020` / `#1e1b4b`, digital clock in red 7-segment style text.

**Arc:**
1. 11:58 PM, 5 %. A text from your own number: "Don't open the door at midnight." Second text: "They'll knock 3 times." Midnight: knock, knock, knock; a voice that sounds exactly like you. Cliff: last text — "That's not me."
2. You don't open. The knocking stops. Outside, every streetlight is off and every clock is stuck at 00:00; a neighbour is frozen mid-step. Text: "Find Mia. She's awake too." Cliff: 4 %, and across the street a flashlight blinks back at you.
3. Mia (yellow raincoat) also gets texts from her own number. Everyone else is frozen. The Static appears at the end of the street, copying your voice. Cliff: it says Mia's name — in Mia's voice.
4. Run to the only lit building: the 24-hour corner shop with one charger. The Static blocks the door. Cliff: Mia's phone hits 0 % and she freezes like everyone else.
5. You find out the rule: when a phone dies, its owner is sent back to 11:58 PM… and the texts are from your own future self. Cliff: 1 % — you realise YOU have to write the texts.
6. Finale: you send "Don't open the door at midnight" to your past self; the loop closes, The Static fades, the clock ticks to 00:01, Mia unfreezes and asks "Did you just text yourself?" THE END.
