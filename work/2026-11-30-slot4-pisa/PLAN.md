# The War That Saved the Leaning Tower
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "The Leaning Tower of Pisa is still standing because its builders went to war."
2. "Italy, eleven seventy-three. Pisa starts a grand bell tower."
3. "But the ground is soft clay and sand."
4. "By the third floor, it's already leaning."
5. "Then Pisa goes to war, and the builders leave."
6. "Guess how long they're gone."
7. "Almost a hundred years."
8. "And all that time, the soil underneath settles and gets firmer."
9. "Experts say without that pause, the tower would almost certainly have toppled."
10. "So a war is the reason it's still standing."
