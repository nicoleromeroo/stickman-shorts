# The War That Saved the Leaning Tower
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "The Leaning Tower of Pisa is only still standing because its builders went off to war."
2. "Italy, eleven seventy-three. Pisa starts building a grand bell tower."
3. "But the ground is soft clay and sand, and the foundation is only three metres deep."
4. "By the third floor, the tower is already leaning."
5. "Then Pisa goes to war, and the builders put down their tools."
6. "Guess how long the pause lasts. Comment a number."
7. "Almost a hundred years."
8. "And all that time, the soil under the tower slowly settles and gets firmer."
9. "When work starts again, the builders try to fix the lean by building the new floors at an angle."
10. "It still leans. But experts say, without that long pause, it would almost certainly have toppled."
11. "So the war that stopped the tower is the reason it's still standing."

Visual ideas: Frame 0: tower tipping, worker holding it up; HUD 'SAVED BY A WAR' | Building site 1173, masons | Cutaway: soft ground, shallow foundation | 3 floors leaning | Builders drop tools, march off with flags | HUD guess | Seasons time-lapse over stump | Soil squeezing | Angled floors added | Engineers digging under high side | Expert card, tower wobble | Final: tower standing, push-in
