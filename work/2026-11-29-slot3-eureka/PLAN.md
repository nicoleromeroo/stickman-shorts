# The Bath That Caught a Thief
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "Legend says a king's crown was solved in a bathtub."
2. "Syracuse, long ago. King Hiero gives a goldsmith gold for a crown."
3. "But a rumour spreads: the goldsmith swapped some gold for cheaper silver."
4. "The king asks Archimedes to find out."
5. "Guess: was the crown pure gold?"
6. "Archimedes climbs into a full bath, and the water spills over."
7. "You see, silver takes up more space than the same weight of gold."
8. "A crown with silver would spill more water. It did."
9. "And Archimedes? Legend says he ran home shouting Eureka, and forgot his clothes."

Visual ideas: Frame 0: Archimedes in bathtub, water splashing, golden crown above; HUD 'SOLVED IN A BATH?' | Syracuse palace, king hands gold to goldsmith | Crown on scale balancing gold | Whispering courtiers, goldsmith sneaky | King points at Archimedes | HUD yes/no | Bath overflow | Water level rising close-up | Gold vs silver blocks same weight, silver bigger | Crown in water, more spills | Goldsmith caught, guards | Archimedes running in towel through street, shocked townspeople
