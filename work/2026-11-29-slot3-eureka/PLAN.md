# The Bath That Caught a Thief
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "Legend says a king's crown was solved by a man getting into his bath."
2. "Syracuse, more than two thousand years ago. King Hiero gives a goldsmith gold to make a crown."
3. "The crown weighs exactly as much as the gold."
4. "But a rumour spreads: the goldsmith kept some gold and mixed in cheaper silver."
5. "The king asks Archimedes to find out."
6. "Guess before the end: was the crown pure gold?"
7. "One day Archimedes climbs into a full bath, and the water spills over."
8. "You see, silver is lighter than gold, so the same weight of silver takes up more space."
9. "A crown with silver inside would push out more water than pure gold."
10. "He tests it. It does. The goldsmith is caught."
11. "And Archimedes? Legend says he was so excited, he ran home shouting Eureka, and forgot to put his clothes on."

Visual ideas: Frame 0: Archimedes in bathtub, water splashing, golden crown above; HUD 'SOLVED IN A BATH?' | Syracuse palace, king hands gold to goldsmith | Crown on scale balancing gold | Whispering courtiers, goldsmith sneaky | King points at Archimedes | HUD yes/no | Bath overflow | Water level rising close-up | Gold vs silver blocks same weight, silver bigger | Crown in water, more spills | Goldsmith caught, guards | Archimedes running in towel through street, shocked townspeople
