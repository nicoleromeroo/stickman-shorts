# The Chocolate Bar That Invented the Microwave
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "The microwave oven was invented thanks to a melted chocolate bar."
2. "America, nineteen forty-five. Percy Spencer builds magnetrons, the tubes that power radar."
3. "He left school at twelve and taught himself physics."
4. "One day, next to a running magnetron, he notices the chocolate bar in his pocket has melted."
5. "Most people would just wipe their pocket. Percy gets curious."
6. "He holds popcorn kernels next to the tube. They pop all over the lab."
7. "Guess what he tried next."
8. "An egg. It burst."
9. "You see, microwaves make the water in food spin, and that spinning makes heat."
10. "Two years later, the first microwave oven goes on sale. It's nearly six feet tall."
11. "And its name? The Radarange. A radar machine, built to cook your dinner."
