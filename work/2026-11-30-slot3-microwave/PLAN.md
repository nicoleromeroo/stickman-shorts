# The Chocolate Bar That Invented the Microwave
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "The microwave oven was invented thanks to a melted chocolate bar."
2. "Nineteen forty-five. Percy Spencer builds magnetrons, the tubes that power radar."
3. "One day, next to a running magnetron, the chocolate in his pocket melts."
4. "Most people would wipe their pocket. Percy gets curious."
5. "He holds popcorn kernels near the tube. They pop all over the lab."
6. "Guess what he tried next."
7. "An egg. It burst."
8. "You see, microwaves make the water in food spin, and that makes heat."
9. "Two years later, the first microwave oven goes on sale. It's nearly six feet tall."
10. "Its name? The Radarange."
