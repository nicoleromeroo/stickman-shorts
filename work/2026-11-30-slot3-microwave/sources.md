# Sources — The Chocolate Bar That Invented the Microwave (True, popular story)
- https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/percy-spencer — left grammar school at 12, taught himself calculus and physics from textbooks; Raytheon; mass-produced magnetrons; "noticed that the chocolate bar in his pocket had started to melt"; popcorn next to the magnetron; patent filed 8 Oct 1945; first commercial oven "Radarange" marketed 1947, 6 feet tall, about 750 pounds.
- https://www.history.com/news/microwave-oven-invention — 1945, candy bar melted while testing an active magnetron; popcorn kernels popped; "A follow-up test with an egg resulted in a burst shell"; Radarange 1947, nearly six feet tall, 750 pounds; magnetrons powered Allied radar.
- https://spectrum.ieee.org/a-history-of-the-microwave-oven — melted chocolate (dated 1946 here), popcorn "popped all over his lab"; microwaves make polar molecules, "particularly those in water, to rotate and produce thermal energy"; Radarange named because the magnetron is the microwave source in radar.
- Facts used: True (all three sources tell the candy-bar story without hedging). Year differs (1945 vs 1946): used 1945 (Lemelson patent date + History). 750 lb ≈ 340 kg.
