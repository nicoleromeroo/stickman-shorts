The most important medicine in history was found because a scientist didn't clean up.
London, nineteen twenty-eight. Alexander Fleming goes on holiday.
He leaves a pile of dishes covered in bacteria on his messy bench.
Two weeks later, he comes back.
One dish has a fuzzy mould growing on it.
And all around the mould, the bacteria are gone.
Something in that mould kills germs.
He calls it penicillin.
It becomes the world's first antibiotic and saves millions of lives.
All because one man didn't do his dishes.
