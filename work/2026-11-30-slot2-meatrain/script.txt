On a clear, sunny day in eighteen seventy-six, it rained meat.
In Kentucky, Mrs. Crouch is outside making soap.
Out of a clear sky, chunks of meat start falling, like snowflakes.
They cover a whole field.
Two brave men taste it. Their verdict: mutton, or maybe venison.
Guess where it came from.
Scientists find lung tissue, muscle and cartilage.
The best explanation came from a local doctor: vultures.
When one vulture throws up, the rest of the flock often joins in.
So it was probably vulture vomit. And two men had already tasted it.
