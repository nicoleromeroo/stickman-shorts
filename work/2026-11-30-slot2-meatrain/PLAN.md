# The Day It Rained Meat in Kentucky
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "On a clear, sunny day in eighteen seventy-six, it rained meat."
2. "Bath County, Kentucky. Mrs. Crouch is outside making soap."
3. "Out of a perfectly clear sky, flakes start falling, like large snowflakes."
4. "Except these snowflakes are chunks of meat."
5. "They cover the fences and the ground, across an area the size of a football field."
6. "Two brave local men taste it. Their verdict: mutton, or maybe venison."
7. "Guess before the end: where did it come from?"
8. "Scientists find lung tissue, muscle and cartilage."
9. "So what was it doing in the sky?"
10. "The best explanation came from a local doctor: vultures."
11. "When one vulture gets scared and throws up, the rest of the flock often joins in."
12. "So the meat probably came from a flock of vomiting vultures. And two men had already tasted it."

Visual ideas: Frame 0: farm yard, meat chunks falling from blue sky, woman with soap pot shocked; HUD 'IT RAINED MEAT' | Farmhouse, woman stirring soap pot | Flakes falling, sky clear | Close-up: chunk lands, 'snowflake' | Wide: fences covered | Two men tasting, chewing | HUD guess | Scientist with microscope | Puzzled scientist | Tilt up: vultures in sky | Vultures looking queasy, one goes 'bleh' | Two men's faces turn green, push-in
