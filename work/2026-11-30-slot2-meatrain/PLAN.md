# The Day It Rained Meat in Kentucky
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "On a clear, sunny day in eighteen seventy-six, it rained meat."
2. "In Kentucky, Mrs. Crouch is outside making soap."
3. "Out of a clear sky, chunks of meat start falling, like snowflakes."
4. "They cover a whole field."
5. "Two brave men taste it. Their verdict: mutton, or maybe venison."
6. "Guess where it came from."
7. "Scientists find lung tissue, muscle and cartilage."
8. "The best explanation came from a local doctor: vultures."
9. "When one vulture throws up, the rest of the flock often joins in."
10. "So it was probably vulture vomit. And two men had already tasted it."
