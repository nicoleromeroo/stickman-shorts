# The War Australia Lost to Birds
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "In nineteen thirty-two, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds."
2. "The birds won."
3. "Western Australia. About twenty thousand emus are stomping through the farmers' wheat."
4. "Guess how many soldiers the army sends."
5. "Three. With two machine guns and ten thousand bullets."
6. "On day one, the emus simply scatter in every direction."
7. "So the major bolts a machine gun onto a truck."
8. "But on the rough ground, the emus are faster, and the truck bounces so hard nobody can aim."
9. "You see, the emus split into small groups, and in every group, one keeps watch."
10. "The major said they could face machine guns like tanks."
11. "By December, the army gives up and goes home."
12. "And in parliament, people joked that if anyone deserved medals, it was the emus."
