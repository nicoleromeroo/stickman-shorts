# The War Australia Lost to Birds
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "In nineteen thirty-two, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds."
2. "The birds won."
3. "About twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat."
4. "Guess how many soldiers the army sent."
5. "Three. With two machine guns."
6. "The emus just scattered."
7. "So the major put a gun on a truck."
8. "But the emus ran faster, and the truck bounced too hard to aim."
9. "You see, they moved in small groups, with one emu keeping watch."
10. "By December, the army gave up and went home."
11. "And in parliament, people joked the emus deserved medals."
