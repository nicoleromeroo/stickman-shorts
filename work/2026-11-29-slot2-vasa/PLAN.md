# The Warship That Sank in Minutes
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "Sweden built one of the mightiest warships in the world. It sank in the harbour."
2. "Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. The Vasa carries sixty-four bronze cannons."
3. "In a test, thirty sailors run back and forth across the deck."
4. "She rocks so hard, the admiral stops the test."
5. "Guess: do they let her sail anyway?"
6. "Yes. Thousands watch from the shore."
7. "A gust of wind tips her over, and water pours in through the open gun ports."
8. "Within minutes, she's on the bottom."
9. "You see, she was top-heavy."
10. "Nobody was punished."
11. "The king himself had approved every measurement."
