# The Warship That Sank in Minutes
Visual idea per sentence (shot plan with frames is added at build time).

1. "Sweden built one of the mightiest warships in the world. It sank before it even left the harbour."
2. "Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. The Vasa carries sixty-four bronze cannons."
3. "Before her first voyage, thirty sailors run back and forth across the deck as a test."
4. "She rolls so hard that the admiral stops the test, afraid she'll tip over."
5. "Guess before the end: do they let her sail anyway?"
6. "On the tenth of August, thousands watch from the shore. The guns fire a salute."
7. "Then a gust of wind pushes her over, and water pours in through the open gun ports."
8. "Within minutes, she's on the bottom of the harbour."
9. "You see, she was top-heavy: too much ship above the water, too little below it."
10. "So who got the blame? Nobody."
11. "Because the king himself had approved every measurement."
