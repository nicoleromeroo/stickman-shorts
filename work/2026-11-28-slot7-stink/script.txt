London's sewers exist because politicians couldn't stand the smell.
London, eighteen fifty-eight. A blazing hot summer.
Most of the city's sewage flows straight into the river Thames.
And the river starts to cook.
The smell is so bad, people call it the Great Stink.
Scientists had warned for years. Nobody listened.
But Parliament sits right on the riverbank.
The lawmakers soak their curtains in chemicals. It doesn't work.
In just eighteen days, they approve a giant new sewer system.
The fastest way to change a law? Put a bad smell under the lawmakers' windows.
Could you have lasted one day? Yes or no?
