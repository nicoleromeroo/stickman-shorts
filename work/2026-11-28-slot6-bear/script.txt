One soldier in the Polish army had a rank, a service number, and fur.
In nineteen forty-two, Polish soldiers in Iran adopted an orphaned bear cub.
They named him Wojtek and fed him milk from a bottle.
He grew. And grew.
Then came a problem: no animals allowed on the ship to Italy.
So the soldiers made him a soldier, with his own service number and pay book.
At Monte Cassino, soldiers said he carried heavy crates of artillery shells.
He was promoted to corporal.
But his friends knew the truth.
On quiet days, he always went looking for the empty boxes.
Would you salute him? Yes or no?
