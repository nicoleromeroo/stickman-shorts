Wojtek: Syrian brown bear adopted 1942 in Iran by the 22nd Artillery Supply Company, 2nd Polish Corps; officially enlisted with rank, service number and pay book; carried crates at Monte Cassino (May 1944, debated, at least one eyewitness account); promoted to corporal. Caretaker: "usually he was a lazy thing and looked for the empty boxes to carry. At Cassino, he carried full ones."
