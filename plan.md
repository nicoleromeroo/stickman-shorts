# Topic plan (Nicole, 16 Nov)
The producer runs use this plan first: take the planned topic for the slot and day. Skip it (and pick a fresh
topic the normal way) only if a fact can't be verified, it is now a near-duplicate in `topics_log.md`, or the
news changed (e.g. a launch got moved). Cross a topic off by moving it to "Done" with the date.
Every video gets a **comment prompt** (rules in CLAUDE.md Step 3). The planned question is a suggestion; keep it
honest and answerable in one word.

## Formats from viral references (Nicole, 16 Nov) — take the format, never copy the content
- "An Unstoppable Spear vs an Unbreakable Shield" (Ricks Clips): **paradox / impossible matchup** → WHAT IF slot.
  Ideas: unstoppable force vs immovable object, "What if you fell into a tunnel through the Earth?", "Can you
  ever reach the horizon?", "Is there a biggest number?". Comment prompt: "Which one wins?"
- "The Instinct That Makes You Crash" (Ricks Clips, 0:59): **a hidden instinct in YOUR brain/body** → FACTS slot.
  Ideas: why you jerk awake when falling asleep (hypnic jerk), phantom phone vibrations, why you can't stop
  looking at a crash, why you forget why you walked into a room (doorway effect), why music gets stuck in your head.
- WARNING (checked the video 16 Nov): that "egg selling" Short opens with "Why are all Jews so rich?" and "Jewish
  finance principles". Its reach comes from an ethnic stereotype. NEVER use group stereotypes as a hook (against our
  rules and YouTube's hate-speech / ad-friendly rules). Only its craft is usable: a question that stays at the top
  the whole video, a small topic badge, word-by-word captions with one yellow word, a warm cream background,
  numbered characters with name tags, and a new label popping in every ~1 s ("4 SONS", "$100 x 4", "STARTUP CAPITAL").
- "How $100 Became $204,984: The Egg Selling Mindset" (The Curious Plot): **money told as a story** with one
  character and an oddly specific final number → FINANCE slot. Our version stays true: real maths, sourced
  assumptions, no get-rich promises (e.g. "How 3 € a day became X €", X = the exact Python result).
- OUR VERSION of the egg parable (finance slot, honest): "4 siblings get 100 € each. Only one ends up rich. Which one
  are you?" Sibling 1 hides it (loses value to inflation, real Destatis/ECB rate), 2 spends it, 3 invests it (sourced
  ~6 %, Python maths, paid-in vs final), 4 grows it with a small business story (clearly labelled "example numbers").
  Each step gets a concept stamp (INFLATION, COMPOUND INTEREST, ORGANIC GROWTH) and a formula that pops in.
  Comment prompt: "Which sibling are you? 1, 2, 3 or 4". No borrowing/leverage advice for teens.
- Note: the 59 s video shows length is fine if a story keeps pulling; stay with 30–45 s unless the story needs more.

- "The Difference Between Rich And Poor" (KnoxClips7000, ~124k likes, 1.6k comments, 0:38): hook = a universal
  question ("Why do the rich get richer and the poor poorer?") over a huge pile of gold, then a THOUGHT EXPERIMENT
  reset: a giant hand takes away all wealth, everyone sits in an empty barrel, each gets ONE chicken. Person 1 eats it
  that night, 2 keeps a hen for eggs, 3 breeds… = consume vs. keep an asset. Craft: characters fill the frame
  (close-ups), detailed backgrounds, camera moves, a chalkboard chart "RICH up / POOR down", 1–2 caption words.
  Lesson: the viral money formula is "same start, different choices" + one reset/what-if. Use it for the 4 siblings
  video and later (e.g. "Everyone gets 1,000 € tomorrow. Where is it after 10 years?").
