## slot4 — The War Australia Lost to Birds

In nineteen thirty-two, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds.
The birds won.
About twenty thousand emus were eating the farmers' wheat.
Guess how many soldiers the army sent.
Three. With two machine guns.
The emus just scattered.
So the major put a gun on a truck.
But the emus ran faster, and the truck bounced too hard to aim.
You see, they moved in small groups, with one emu keeping watch.
By December, the army gave up and went home.
And in parliament, people joked the emus deserved medals.

## slot2 — The Warship That Sank in Minutes

Sweden built one of the mightiest warships in the world. It sank in the harbour.
Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. The Vasa carries sixty-four bronze cannons.
In a test, thirty sailors run back and forth across the deck.
She rocks so hard, the admiral stops the test.
Guess: do they let her sail anyway?
Yes. Thousands watch from the shore.
A gust of wind tips her over, and water pours in through the open gun ports.
Within minutes, she's on the bottom.
You see, she was top-heavy.
Nobody was punished.
The king himself had approved every measurement.

## slot3 — The Bath That Caught a Thief

Legend says a king's crown was solved in a bathtub.
Syracuse, long ago. King Hiero gives a goldsmith gold for a crown.
But a rumour spreads: the goldsmith swapped some gold for cheaper silver.
The king asks Archimedes to find out.
Guess: was the crown pure gold?
Archimedes climbs into a full bath, and the water spills over.
You see, silver takes up more space than the same weight of gold.
A crown with silver would spill more water. It did.
And Archimedes? Legend says he ran home shouting Eureka, and forgot his clothes.
