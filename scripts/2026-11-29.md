## slot4 — The War Australia Lost to Birds

In nineteen thirty-two, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds.
The birds won.
Western Australia. About twenty thousand emus are stomping through the farmers' wheat.
Guess how many soldiers the army sends.
Three. With two machine guns and ten thousand bullets.
On day one, the emus simply scatter in every direction.
So the major bolts a machine gun onto a truck.
But on the rough ground, the emus are faster, and the truck bounces so hard nobody can aim.
You see, the emus split into small groups, and in every group, one keeps watch.
The major said they could face machine guns like tanks.
By December, the army gives up and goes home.
And in parliament, people joked that if anyone deserved medals, it was the emus.

## slot2 — The Warship That Sank in Minutes

Sweden built one of the mightiest warships in the world. It sank before it even left the harbour.
Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. The Vasa carries sixty-four bronze cannons.
Before her first voyage, thirty sailors run back and forth across the deck as a test.
She rolls so hard that the admiral stops the test, afraid she'll tip over.
Guess before the end: do they let her sail anyway?
On the tenth of August, thousands watch from the shore. The guns fire a salute.
Then a gust of wind pushes her over, and water pours in through the open gun ports.
Within minutes, she's on the bottom of the harbour.
You see, she was top-heavy: too much ship above the water, too little below it.
So who got the blame? Nobody.
Because the king himself had approved every measurement.

## slot3 — The Bath That Caught a Thief

Legend says a king's crown was solved by a man getting into his bath.
Syracuse, more than two thousand years ago. King Hiero gives a goldsmith gold to make a crown.
The crown weighs exactly as much as the gold.
But a rumour spreads: the goldsmith kept some gold and mixed in cheaper silver.
The king asks Archimedes to find out.
Guess before the end: was the crown pure gold?
One day Archimedes climbs into a full bath, and the water spills over.
You see, silver is lighter than gold, so the same weight of silver takes up more space.
A crown with silver inside would push out more water than pure gold.
He tests it. It does. The goldsmith is caught.
And Archimedes? Legend says he was so excited, he ran home shouting Eureka, and forgot to put his clothes on.
